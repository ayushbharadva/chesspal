# Naming shortlist, 22 October 2026

The preferred direction is **Unfold Chess**: a friendly promise that the reasons
behind moves become understandable. Every proposed name below contains one or
two words. The app has not been renamed; the owner will choose first.

## Recommended choices

1. **Unfold Chess** — strongest overall fit. Easy to say, clearly identifies the
   subject, and supports a visual identity based on revealing ideas step by step.
2. **Chess Unpacked** — strongest new two-word alternative. Plain language that
   suggests explaining a position and breaking down its choices.
3. **Plylens** — strongest new one-word option. A lens on each move; short enough
   for an app icon and a distinct brand. Beginners may need the subtitle
   “Understand every move” because “ply” is chess terminology.
4. **Chess Untangled** — especially suited to making confusing positions and
   opening branches approachable.
5. **Chess Unscripted** — strongest fit for the product's transfer exercises:
   learn ideas well enough to use them beyond a memorized example.

## Preliminary collision screening

Broad web searches used quoted names, followed by indexed Google Play and Apple
App Store searches for the leading options. These are preliminary discovery
checks, not a claim that a name, domain, handle or trademark is available. Search
engines can omit new, regional, removed or unindexed products. No domain has been
registered or reserved.

No obvious exact-name chess learning app surfaced for Unfold Chess, Chess
Unpacked, Chess Unveiled, Chess Untangled, Plylens, Plypath, Plyscope, UnfoldChess
or UnveilChess in these searches. Plypath also occurs as a software variable;
Plyscope has unrelated industrial search results. This is a reason to investigate
further after choosing finalists, rather than evidence of exclusive use.

**Chess Unfolded has a concrete related use:** the [Watch Chess App Store
listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/watch-chess/id980989211) includes an event
with that title. The app itself is named Watch Chess. Styling our name as
`chess.unfolded` would not remove the wording overlap, and that styling does not
establish ownership of a matching web address.

Several attractive alternatives were dropped because existing uses surfaced:

- [Chessfold](https://www.chessfold.com/): existing chess product.
- [Chessplain](https://getchessplain.com/): existing chess analysis product.
- [Plywise](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@plywise/chessboard): existing chessboard software.
- [Rookwise](https://rookwiseai.com/): existing education app.
- [Plyloom](https://plyloom.ru/): existing knowledge-mapping software.
- [Plybloom](https://www.etsy.com/shop/Plybloom): existing shop.
- [Quiet Tempo](https://apps.apple.com/ch/app/quiet-tempo/id6809381580): existing app.
- [Sapling](https://github.com/Timmoth/Sapling): existing chess engine, making
  Chess Sapling less distinctive within chess.

Choose two or three finalists before the deeper domain, social-handle and
trademark screening. For the product being built now, **Unfold Chess** remains my
overall recommendation, **Chess Unpacked** the clearest fresh alternative, and
**Plylens** the best one-word candidate.

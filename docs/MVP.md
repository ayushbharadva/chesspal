# ChessPal MVP

Audience: beginners, from players who have never moved a piece to those who know the rules and want puzzles and opening ideas ([decision 0010](decisions/0010-learn-course.md)).

## Implemented

- **Practice:** 5-puzzle sessions from 3,200 bundled Lichess puzzles, progressive hints, solution replay, feedback, more practice and review of assisted attempts.
- **Wrong-move replies:** a wrong puzzle move stays on the board, the opponent's engine-checked reply follows and its square turns red, and one line says why (it checkmates, takes a piece, lets the king escape, or the opponent is safe now). Back one move and Reset are icon buttons; the mistake still counts ([decision 0012](decisions/0012-wrong-move-replies.md)).
- **Learn course:** a fifth tab with one locked path of 84 short lessons in four sections (Learn the game, Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced; 21 units), from the board and how each piece moves to rook endgames and planning ([plan](LEARN-PLAN.md)). Each lesson is 3 to 9 steps with at least three things to do on a board (each puzzle counts): read, choose an answer, tap squares, collect stars with a piece, take every piece safely, play a move or line, beat a simple defender (basic mates and pawn races) or solve four bundled puzzles, chosen so lessons do not repeat them. Finishing a lesson opens the next and earns one to three stars from mistakes and hints; replays can only raise them. A finished lesson counts as a practice day. Progress shows lessons and stars.
- **First run and navigation:** a welcome screen with three one-line bullets, then "How well do you play?", where "I'm new to chess" opens lesson one at the Beginner level. Five bottom tabs: Home (the next Learn lesson leads until Section 1 is finished, then today's puzzles, review, daily challenge, next opening step, this week), Learn, Puzzles (level, mixed practice, review, daily challenge, skills, square trainer), Openings and Progress. Openings are hidden for now (the tab, Home's opening step and Progress's opening stats) behind `SHOW_OPENINGS` in `src/constants/features.ts`; their screens and saved progress stay. Rounds, lessons and settings open above the tabs. Copy stays short and avoids jargon ([decision 0007](decisions/0007-first-run-tabs-plain-words.md)).
- **Levels:** the player picks Beginner, Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert once on first launch (changeable from Puzzles, Progress or Settings; [decision 0008](decisions/0008-hard-and-expert-levels.md)). Fresh puzzles in a round are a fifth one level easier, a fifth one level harder and the rest at level (1/3/1 of five), easiest first, after up to three due reviews. One at-level slot goes to the player's weakest theme when there is one. Changing level switches a practice round's unstarted puzzles at once; skill rounds and the daily challenge keep their level. Each practice puzzle shows its level.
- **Skills:** eight tactic themes in two units (checkmate patterns; winning material), each with an original concept card and "look for" cue. Three five-puzzle rounds per skill: a warm-up one level below with the theme named, a round at level without the label, and a test one level above that hides two puzzles from other themes. Four of five independent solves clear a round; each try uses fresh puzzles; a skill is mastered only after its test round. Progress is kept for each level. Skill puzzles feed the attempt log, reviews, themes and streak.
- **Strengths and weaknesses:** per-theme performance adapted from the Lichess puzzle dashboard (average rating − 500 + 1000 × independent rate) over the last 200 attempts. A theme needs five attempts before it is called strong (three independent, above overall) or worth practicing (two assisted, below overall). Shown on Results, Progress and Home.
- **Daily challenge** (the daily route in code): five puzzles a day from one rotating theme, easiest first. Before puzzles 2 and 4 the player picks a normal puzzle (2 points, or 1 with help) or a hard one (4 points without help, otherwise nothing). One scored run a day, compared with the player's usual score (the median of earlier runs) once three exist. Challenge puzzles feed the attempt log, reviews, themes and streak.
- **Square trainer** (board vision in code): 30-second rounds of finding named squares from White's or Black's side; the last 20 scores per side are kept on the device. Rounds do not count toward the practice streak.
- **Recall reviews:** the existing attempt log schedules next-day review, then 3/7/14/30-day intervals after independent due recalls. Assistance resets to next day; same-day or early successes do not postpone review. Standard rounds reserve three due positions while fresh content is available, then use remaining due positions before future familiar ones when needed. Review rounds include due puzzles and immediate assisted retries, up to five per round.
- **Next learning step:** Home offers to restart an unfinished lesson first, then surfaces the oldest due lesson recall. With no unfinished or due lesson it suggests an untried application exercise or opening branch, following the most recently practiced guide. Recommendations, due reviews and practice activity refresh at local midnight and when returning to the foreground. Demonstrations do not create attempts; all examples remain available on the Openings tab.
- **Learn:** Italian Game, Caro-Kann Defence and Queen's Gambit. Each guide has three example branches, move explanations, plans, common mistakes and demonstration playback. First practice shows move guidance; later recall hides the answer and move notes. Asking for a hint or revisiting the demonstration marks that attempt assisted.
- **Apply and recall:** two original unfamiliar positions per guide ask learners to use a stated opening idea. Goal-specific answers are checked for legality and engine soundness. Branch and exercise attempts use the same delayed review intervals as puzzles, with a separate due queue on Learn. Immediate repetition does not establish delayed retention.
- **Progress:** independent and assisted solves and lesson recalls, completed workouts, practiced guides, weekly activity and streaks. Completed workouts and lesson attempts both count as practice days, with each local date counted once; viewing a demonstration alone does not count. Rounds resume at the puzzle the player reached; a puzzle, Learn lesson or opening lesson left part-way (back to Home or by closing the app) starts again from its beginning, keeping its help, mistakes and slips ([decision 0011](decisions/0011-restart-unfinished-items.md)). Selected branches and demonstration positions are kept. Existing puzzle history and guide progress are preserved; old guide completion does not count as an independent lesson recall.
- **Board:** legal tap-to-move and drag-to-move interaction, sliding moves, a short pause before the opponent's reply, a shake on a wrong move that has no stored reply, a marked king in check, orientation, move highlights, accessible square labels, castling, en passant and explicit promotion choices.
- **Sound effects:** six quiet sounds: move, capture, check and checkmate on the board, including replies and demonstrations, plus a soft tone for an independent solve and for a wrong move. All are original, follow the phone's silent switch and leave other audio playing. Improving them is on the to-do list ([decision 0006](decisions/0006-sound-effects.md)).
- **Settings:** system, light or dark appearance, a choice of three piece sets (Classic by default, Modern, Wood; [decision 0009](decisions/0009-piece-sets.md)), optional haptic feedback and optional sound effects, all saved on the device. Motion follows the device's reduce-motion setting.
- **Offline:** all learning content and progress stay on the device; no account or backend is needed.

The existing Expo SDK 57 foundation is retained. Production builds and physical-device validation are release gates; an app-store submission additionally needs the owner's Expo/Google Play accounts and signing setup.

## Content

Lichess puzzle data is CC0. The importer selects rated, popular short puzzles; the developer-only Stockfish 19 Lite pipeline checks each learner move at depth 14 before bundling. The bundle holds 3,200 puzzles rated 400–1899 across eight themes and five levels, with at least 76 puzzles tagged for every theme at every level. Legality and complete lines are also checked by tests.

The Learn course is original: positions and words are ChessPal's own, with structure ideas from the open-source Lichess Learn ([decision 0010](decisions/0010-learn-course.md)). Tests check every lesson against chess.js; from Section 2, `npm run content:verify-course` checks each answer and scripted reply with Stockfish 19 Lite at depth 14 ([details](content-pipeline.md)).

The three guides are sourced from Chess.com's opening references, with concise original explanations. They demonstrate example lines, not mandatory replies in every game. [Content details](content-pipeline.md).

The six added branches also use the pinned official Lichess opening dataset. Six original transfer exercises were checked with Stockfish 19 Lite at depth 14, accepting answers within 35 centipawns of its unrestricted root score. Answers meet the exercise's stated goal; other sound moves may exist. [Opening practice sources and verification](../content/OPENING-PRACTICE-SOURCES.md).

## Verify and run

Use Node 22.13+ or Node 24+. Run `npm ci`, `npm run check`, then `npm start` and open in Expo Go. `eas.json` provides preview APK and production AAB profiles; use `npx eas-cli@latest build --platform android --profile preview` after account/project setup.

Shareable race codes, placement tests, broader course libraries, game imports, multiplayer, accounts, subscriptions and AI coaching are outside this release. Add features after testing the two learning loops with users.

Research direction and evidence: [21 October investigation](research/2026-10-21-chess-learning.md).
The review intervals are a transparent pilot rule, not a validated personalized memory model.
